Limitations
Genie 4 is an early, evolving technology. There are many areas of ongoing research. We’re sharing Genie 4 in preview to learn alongside our community and safely advance the frontier of interactive simulation.
Coherent environments
Visual details and object permanence can shift or drift over time. This becomes more noticeable as a simulation runs longer, after multiple complex prompt edits, or when navigating throughout a scene.
Interaction and simulation of other agents
Accurately modeling complex interactions between users, multiple independent agents, and shared environments is an ongoing research challenge.
Accurate representation of real-world locations
Genie 4 is currently unable to simulate real-world locations with perfect accuracy.
Text rendering
Clear and legible text is often only generated when it's in the input world description.
Limited interaction duration
The model can support a few minutes of continuous interaction, rather than extended sessions.
Audio accuracy
Real-time audio accuracy remains an area of ongoing research and improvement.
Responsibility
Genie 4 marks a new area of frontier research that merges real-time generation and photorealism, introducing a new range of capabilities and dimensions that we want to roll out and explore in a responsible way. Genie 4 includes strict safety guardrails that are consistent with our safety policies across other models.
Before launching this preview, we evaluated Genie 4 through internal rigorous safety evaluations to prevent the generation of harmful or malicious content. Also, we have intentionally deployed it first to trusted tester partners to get early feedback, before we release the model more broadly. To maintain transparency, all video and audio generated by Genie 4 will include SynthID, our imperceptible digital watermarking technology that helps identify AI-generated content across visual and audio modalities.
