## Intelligence, allocated to the work

FUMO Lab is a frontier AI lab based in Singapore. We build **Fusion**: a
compound intelligence system that allocates model capability to the
uncertainty that matters at each step of a task, then returns one authoritative
answer or action.

Fusion is designed around four properties:

- **State-aware allocation** — new observations can change the next model,
  role, context view, or compute budget.
- **Complementary capability** — models can contribute as solvers, explorers,
  critics, or verifiers.
- **One authoritative result** — evidence is reconciled before a client-visible
  answer or action is produced.
- **One integration surface** — OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions and
  Responses APIs fit existing clients and agent workflows.
