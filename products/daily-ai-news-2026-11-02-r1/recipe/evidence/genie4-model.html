Genie 4

A new generation of world simulation

Read blog

Genie 4 is our most realistic and interactive world model yet.

Genie 4 incorporates and builds on capabilities from the Gemini family. Gemini Omni anchors simulations in real-world appearance and behavior, while other Gemini models provide the complex reasoning needed to help maintain a simulation’s coherence and continuity.

  • Capabilities
  • Future applications
  • Join waitlist
  • Limitations
  • Responsibility

Shape worlds in real time

Create and change worlds with natural language – live. Describe landscapes and events, move the camera, add new characters, or change the weather. Then see what happens next.

Create realistic interactive environments

Generate environments where objects collide, water flows, and trees sway in the wind. And where generated characters move – and behave – naturally.

Direct complex events

Create worlds with plausible cause-and-effect sequences. Gemini works behind the scenes to interpret prompts, and make dynamic plans within changing worlds.

Hear your worlds

Worlds come alive with natively generated native audio. Leaves crunch underfoot, crowds cheer, and characters can speak with you – and each other.

Future applications

Genie 4’s worlds have the potential for a wide range of real-world applications, from creating playful interactive experiences to generating worlds where agents – like SIMA – can learn new skills.

Frontier robotics research

Experimenting with Genie 4 and Gemini Robotics

Towards world simulation

The ability to explore an unlimited range of realistic environments is a key stepping stone on the path to AGI. Training and testing AI agents in truer-to-life environments brings us closer to their successful deployment in the physical world.

Join Project Genie waitlist

Genie 4 is available as a research preview for trusted testers, and will be coming soon starting with Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio.

Limitations

Genie 4 is an early, evolving technology. There are many areas of ongoing research. We’re sharing Genie 4 in preview to learn alongside our community and safely advance the frontier of interactive simulation.

Coherent environments

Visual details and object permanence can shift or drift over time. This becomes more noticeable as a simulation runs longer, after multiple complex prompt edits, or when navigating throughout a scene.

Interaction and simulation of other agents

Accurately modeling complex interactions between users, multiple independent agents, and shared environments is an ongoing research challenge.

Accurate representation of real-world locations

Genie 4 is currently unable to simulate real-world locations with perfect accuracy.

Text rendering

Clear and legible text is often only generated when it's in the input world description.

Limited interaction duration

The model can support a few minutes of continuous interaction, rather than extended sessions.

Audio accuracy

Real-time audio accuracy remains an area of ongoing research and improvement.

Responsibility

Genie 4 marks a new area of frontier research that merges real-time generation and photorealism, introducing a new range of capabilities and dimensions that we want to roll out and explore in a responsible way. Genie 4 includes strict safety guardrails that are consistent with our safety policies across other models.

Before launching this preview, we evaluated Genie 4 through internal rigorous safety evaluations to prevent the generation of harmful or malicious content. Also, we have intentionally deployed it first to trusted tester partners to get early feedback, before we release the model more broadly. To maintain transparency, all video and audio generated by Genie 4 will include SynthID, our imperceptible digital watermarking technology that helps identify AI-generated content across visual and audio modalities.
