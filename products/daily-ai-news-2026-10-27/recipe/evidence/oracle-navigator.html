        Explore Oracle AI Success Navigator features and capabilities

        Starter Configurations

        Jump-start your cloud implementation with a Starter Configuration, a ready-to-use test environment that can help you streamline design and deployment.

        • Reduce implementation effort and increase speed to value with out-of-the-box business processes and Starter Configurations to help address your organization's needs.
        • Start exploring with preconfigured user personas based on key roles.
        • Apply proven best practices and actionable insights to enhance implementation and decision-making.
        • Access preconfigured KPIs, analyses, dashboards, and reports for data-driven insights.

        Milestone assessments

        Monitor progress with clear entry and exit criteria for each phase of the journey, leveraging self-guided assessments to proactively address potential risks.

        • Track implementation progress with defined success criteria by implementation phase.
        • Conduct self-guided assessments to measure quality and mitigate risks.
        • Retake assessments at any point to update progress and refine your implementation approach.

        Milestone tracking

        Align teams, implementation partners, and Oracle with milestones that drive coordinated action and project success—now enhanced by Ask Oracle, which highlights quality benchmarks, process improvements, and potential risks to support better planning and improve implementation outcomes.

        • View and manage program and project milestones.
        • Update milestones as needed and add new ones as needed.
        • Include key program and project details, such as name, status, and implementation partner.
        • Assign key team members to tasks for improved visibility and collaboration.

        Feature innovation

        Stay informed with a comprehensive list of new features that are dynamically recommended by Ask Oracle to match your production profile and business objectives. Ask Oracle streamlines discovery and highlights where to focus next, enabling faster adoption and greater value.

        • Access a tailored feature list with detailed information about past, current, and soon-to-be-released features.
        • Navigate innovations categorized by pillar, business goal, and key attributes.
        • View tailored learning content for each feature as well as release notes, the steps required to enable new features, and best practices.

        Release readiness, adoption roadmaps, and release guidance

        Easily prioritize and plan new capabilities—with Ask Oracle providing tailored recommendations for feature selection, actionable next steps, and real-time updates—to align your roadmap with your evolving business needs.

        • Plan how to implement new features using a list or grid view.
        • Easily prioritize, edit the status of, and assign users and due dates to features to track progress.
        • Review release schedules and strategy recommendations for your upgrade plan, including guidance on delivered features as well as pre-upgrade blackout dates.

        Oracle Modern Best Practice, key features, and AI Adoption Center

        Access expert resources, learning tools, guidance, and a best practice framework to efficiently adopt high-impact innovations.

        • Follow a repeatable process for modernizing your business processes with AI innovations in Fusion Applications with every quarterly release.
        • Explore by key themes such as the Oracle Redwood UI evolution and artificial intelligence.
        • Review Oracle Modern Best Practice and Key Features for process optimization opportunities and to keep your Oracle Cloud solution operating efficiently as you innovate.
